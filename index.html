Privacy Policy for Cyber Space Wave

Developer: RV Apps Studio • Package: com.rv15appstudio.cyberspacewave • Effective Date: October 8, 2026
Summary: Cyber Space Wave is an offline-first arcade game. We do not collect, track, or share any personal identifying information (PII). All progress, scores, and unlockables are stored locally on your device.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how RV Apps Studio ("we", "us", or "our") manages your information when you download, install, and play Cyber Space Wave on Android devices.

2. Information Collection and Storage

Cyber Space Wave does not collect or transmit any personally identifiable information.

  • Gameplay Progression: Completed levels, star ratings, crystals collected, unlocked ship skins, and custom trails are saved exclusively on your local device storage via Android SharedPreferences.
  • Settings Preferences: Audio toggles (sound effects, background synthwave music) and haptic vibration preferences are kept strictly local.
  • No Cloud Accounts: The game does not require account registration, passwords, email addresses, or cloud logins.

3. Device Permissions Used

Cyber Space Wave requires minimal system access:

  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used solely to provide tactile haptic feedback during gameplay collisions, boost gate activations, and crystal pickups. This permission does not access personal data.

We do not request or use sensitive permissions such as Camera, Location, Contacts, Microphone, or External File Storage.

4. Third-Party Services & Tracking

The current version of Cyber Space Wave does not integrate third-party tracking SDKs, data brokers, or advertising networks. If future updates introduce optional features (such as Google Play Games Leaderboards or AdMob), this policy will be promptly updated and transparent consent will be requested.

5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Cyber Space Wave is designed for universal audiences of all ages. Because we collect zero personal information, our application is fully compliant with the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policy.

6. Data Security

Since your gameplay data remains confined to your device's sandbox storage, your data cannot be intercepted through our servers. Uninstalling the application will delete your locally saved progression data in accordance with Android OS standards.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect app updates or regulatory adjustments. Any modifications will be reflected on this page with an updated Effective Date.
